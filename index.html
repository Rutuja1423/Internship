# Step 3c: Verify & Correct TotalPrice = Quantity * UnitPrice
df['Calculated_Total'] = df['Quantity'] * df['UnitPrice']
df['Price_Diff'] = abs(df['TotalPrice'] - df['Calculated_Total'])

# Impute missing CouponCode with 'NoCoupon'
df['CouponCode'] = df['CouponCode'].fillna('NoCoupon')

# Recalculate TotalPrice where discrepancies exist
df.loc[df['Price_Diff'] > 0.01, 'TotalPrice'] = df['Calculated_Total']

Quality Control Checklist

  • Missing Value Treatment

    Filled missing `CouponCode` entries with `'NoCoupon'` rather than dropping, preserving discount usage intent.

  • Duplicate OrderID Removal

    Audited primary keys against `ORDxxxxxx` regex pattern to eliminate accidental duplicate order records.

  • Date Format & Tracking Validation

    Converted all dates to ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` standard and verified `TRKxxxxxxxx` tracking number formatting.

  • Pricing Consistency Audit

    Reconciled row-level math (`TotalPrice = Quantity * UnitPrice`) to eliminate upstream billing glitches.
